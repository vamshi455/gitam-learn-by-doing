# Open Electives 🌐

**Open Electives** are your chance to explore beyond the usual CS path. This is where you get to be curious, try new things, and even step into other fields.

## How it works
You take **24 credits** of open electives in total, split as:
- **9 credits from within your own School** (the CS options listed below), and
- **15 credits from *other* schools** — like management, design, science, or humanities.

Studying outside your comfort zone makes you a more creative and well-rounded engineer. Some of the best ideas come from mixing two different worlds!

## Go Further: Minors and Honors ✨
Want to stand out even more? You have extra options:

- **Minor programmes (18 extra credits):** Take a focused set of courses in another area — like AI, Data Science, or even a non-CS field — and earn an official *Minor* alongside your degree. It's a great way to build a second strength.
- **Honors:** For students who want to dig deeper into research and advanced topics in their own field and graduate with an *Honors* tag.

These are optional and mean extra work, but they make your degree richer and your resume stronger.
