## 🧠 What this subject is about
Natural Language Processing, or NLP, is about helping computers understand human language, like English or Hindi. Since computers only know numbers, this subject teaches you how to turn words into numbers the computer can work with. It is how machines learn to read, listen, and reply.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
NLP powers chatbots, Google Translate, voice assistants like Alexa, and even the spam detector in your email. The chat AI everyone talks about today is built on NLP ideas. Learning it lets you build tools that understand and respond to real human words.

## 🎯 A practical real-world problem to learn it
Let's build a "Movie Review Detector" that reads a review and decides if it is happy (positive) or unhappy (negative). Imagine hundreds of reviews for a new film, and the studio wants to know if people liked it without reading them all. First you break each review into words, a step called tokenization. Then you teach the computer that words like "amazing" lean positive and "boring" lean negative. Your program reads a new review and gives its verdict. This is called sentiment analysis, and companies use it every day to understand customer feelings.

## 🛠️ What you'd build or try
- Collect a small set of movie reviews labelled positive or negative
- Break each review into words using tokenization
- Turn words into numbers with simple embeddings or word counts
- Train a classifier to predict the mood of a brand new review

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Tokenization
- Word embeddings
- Sentiment analysis
- Text classification
- Language models
