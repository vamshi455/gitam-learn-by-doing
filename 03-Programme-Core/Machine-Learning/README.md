# Machine Learning (24CSEN2161)

**Category:** Programme Core &middot; **Credits:** 4 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 3-0-2-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
Machine Learning is teaching computers to learn from examples instead of being told every rule. Show a computer many photos of cats and dogs, and it slowly learns to tell them apart on its own. This subject teaches you how that learning actually happens.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Machine Learning is behind Netflix recommendations, spam filters, face unlock, and medical tools that spot diseases. It is one of the most exciting and well-paid areas in tech right now. As an AI and ML student, this subject is the core skill your whole degree is built around.

## 🎯 A practical real-world problem to learn it
Let's build a program that predicts a student's exam score based on how many hours they studied. You collect some real data: past students, their study hours, and their marks. Your model looks at this data and learns the pattern, like "more study usually means higher marks". Then you give it a new number, say 5 hours, and it predicts a score. Next, level up to a classifier that decides pass or fail. You will split your data into practice and test sets and check how often your model is right. This is real machine learning, start to finish.

## 🛠️ What you'd build or try
- Collect a simple dataset of study hours and exam scores
- Train a regression model to predict scores from study time
- Build a classifier that predicts pass or fail
- Test your model on new data and measure its accuracy

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Supervised and unsupervised learning
- Regression
- Classification
- Clustering
- Model evaluation
