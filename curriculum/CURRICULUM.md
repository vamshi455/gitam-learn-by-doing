# 📘 GITAM Curriculum — B.Tech CSE (AI & ML)

**Programme code:** UCSEN03 · **School:** GITAM School of Computer Science and Engineering
**Effective:** 2025–26 admitted batch · **Duration:** 4 years · **Total credits:** 160

*(A student-friendly summary of the official curriculum. The full, authoritative
document is GITAM's own PDF; regulations live at
https://www.gitam.edu/academics/academic-regulations )*

---

## 🎯 What this degree is trying to make you

The programme wants graduates who can:
- **PEO 1** — solve real-world challenges with strong AI & ML foundations
- **PEO 2** — build practical, data-driven solutions using the right tools & algorithms
- **PEO 3** — work with ethics, responsibility, and a habit of lifelong learning

This repo exists to serve exactly that spirit: **learn each subject by solving a real problem.**

## 🧱 How the 160 credits are built

| # | Category | Code | Min Credits | Share |
|---|----------|------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | University Core | UC | 19 | 12% |
| 2 | Faculty Core | FC | 55 | 34% |
| 3 | Programme Core | PC | 50 | 31% |
| 4 | Programme Electives | PE | 12 | 8% |
| 5 | Open Electives | OE | 24 | 15% |
| | **Total** | | **160** | **100%** |

**L-T-P-S-J** on each course means **L**ecture · **T**utorial · **P**ractical ·
**S**kill · **J**ob-training/Project hours per week. **C** = Credits.
