# Personal Financial Planning (FINA1081)

**Category:** University Core &middot; **Credits:** 1 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 1-0-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject teaches you how to take care of your own money so it grows instead of disappearing. You will learn how to make a budget, save regularly, and understand how interest can either help you or hurt you. It is a life skill that school usually forgets to teach.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Everyone earns and spends money, but very few people learn how to manage it well. Understanding compound interest is why some people retire comfortably while others struggle, and knowing about loans stops you from drowning in credit card debt. Even a small amount saved every month, invested wisely, can grow into a large sum over the years thanks to compounding.

## 🎯 A practical real-world thing to try
Build a simple "money grows" calculator in Python or a spreadsheet. Let the user enter how much they save each month, an interest rate, and a number of years, then show how much they'd have at the end. Try 500 rupees a month at 8% for 30 years and watch how compound growth makes the total much bigger than what you put in. Change the numbers and see how starting just 5 years earlier makes a huge difference. This one experiment often changes how students think about saving forever.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Making and sticking to a budget
- Building a saving habit
- How simple and compound interest work
- Basics of investments and where money can grow
- Seeing the power of compound growth over time
