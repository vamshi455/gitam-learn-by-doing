# Environmental Studies (ENVS1003)

**Category:** University Core &middot; **Credits:** 3 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 3-0-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject is about understanding the natural world we live in and how humans affect it. You will learn how ecosystems work, what causes pollution, and why the climate is changing. Most importantly, you learn what "sustainability" means and how we can live without destroying the planet.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
The air we breathe, the water we drink, and the food we eat all come from the environment, so keeping it healthy is not optional. Cities flood, forests disappear, and heatwaves get worse when we ignore these problems. Engineers today are asked to design solar panels, clean water systems, and low-waste products, so understanding the environment makes you a more responsible and useful builder.

## 🎯 A practical real-world thing to try
For one week, keep a simple diary of your own environmental footprint. Note how much plastic you throw away, how much water you use, and how you travel each day. At the end of the week, pick just one habit to change, like carrying a reusable bottle or switching off unused lights, and track how much waste or energy you save. You will be surprised how small changes add up, and you will understand sustainability by living it instead of just reading about it.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- How ecosystems and food chains work
- Causes and effects of air, water, and land pollution
- What sustainability really means
- Climate change and why it happens
- Conservation of natural resources and biodiversity
