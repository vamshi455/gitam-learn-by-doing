# Fundamentals of Entrepreneurship (IENT1051)

**Category:** University Core &middot; **Credits:** 2 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 2-0-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject is about turning an idea into a real business that people actually want. You will learn how to check if your idea is any good, who your customers are, and how a company makes money. It is less about being rich and more about solving real problems in a smart way.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Almost every app, shop, and startup you use began as one person's idea. Companies like Zomato or Ola started because someone spotted a problem and built a solution people would pay for. Even if you never start a company, knowing how businesses work helps you get hired, pitch your ideas at work, and understand the world of money and products around you.

## 🎯 A practical real-world thing to try
Think of one small problem that annoys students at your college, like long canteen queues or confusing timetables. Imagine a simple product or app that fixes it. Now go talk to 10 real students and ask if they'd actually use it and pay for it. Sketch the cheapest possible version (an MVP) you could build to test the idea, then write a 60-second pitch explaining it. You will learn more from those 10 conversations than from any textbook.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Validating an idea before wasting time building it
- Understanding business models and how money flows
- Getting to know your customers deeply
- Building a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- Pitching your idea clearly and confidently
