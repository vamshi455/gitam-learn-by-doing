# The Indian Constitution (POLS1051)

**Category:** University Core &middot; **Credits:** 1 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 1-0-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject is about the rulebook of our country, the Indian Constitution. You will learn about the rights that protect you, the duties you have as a citizen, and how the government is supposed to run. It explains why India is a democracy and how laws are made and applied fairly.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
The Constitution is the reason you can speak freely, vote, and be treated equally no matter where you come from. When people know their rights, they can stand up against unfair treatment, and when they know their duties, society works better for everyone. Understanding it helps you become an informed citizen who can question wrong decisions and take part in democracy instead of just watching from the side.

## 🎯 A practical real-world thing to try
Pick one Fundamental Right, like the Right to Equality or the Right to Freedom of Speech, and find a real news story where that right was in the spotlight. Read what happened, then write a short note explaining which right was involved, who was protected, and whether you think it was handled fairly. Doing this a few times shows you that the Constitution is not just an old document but a living tool that affects real people every single day.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Fundamental Rights that protect citizens
- Fundamental Duties every citizen holds
- How governance and the three branches of government work
- What democracy means and how it functions
- How laws are made and applied
