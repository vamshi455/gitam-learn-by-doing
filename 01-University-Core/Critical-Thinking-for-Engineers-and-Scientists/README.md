# Critical Thinking for Engineers and Scientists (24CSEN3321)

**Category:** University Core &middot; **Credits:** 2 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 2-0-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject teaches you how to think clearly and carefully before you decide anything. You will learn to break big, messy problems into smaller pieces, ask good questions, and check whether the "facts" you hear are actually true. It is like giving your brain a toolkit so you don't get fooled or confused.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Engineers and scientists make decisions that affect real people, so thinking clearly really matters. When a bridge is designed, a medicine is tested, or an app suggests news to millions of users, someone has to ask "Is this evidence strong? Are we being fair? What could go wrong?" People who think critically catch mistakes early, avoid scams, and don't fall for fake news or biased advice.

## 🎯 A practical real-world thing to try
Pick any viral claim you saw online this week, like "this food cures a disease" or "this phone lasts 3 days on one charge." Write down the claim, then hunt for the actual evidence behind it. Ask: Who said this? What proof did they give? Could they be biased or trying to sell something? Then write one short paragraph deciding whether you believe it and why. Do this a few times and you will start spotting weak arguments everywhere.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Logical reasoning and spotting when an argument doesn't add up
- Framing a problem clearly before jumping to solutions
- Judging the strength and source of evidence
- Recognizing and avoiding your own biases
- Structured, step-by-step thinking
