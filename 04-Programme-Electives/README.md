# Programme Electives 🎓

Welcome to your **Programme Electives**! These are the courses that let you shape your own path inside Computer Science. Everyone learns the core, but here *you* get to choose what excites you most.

## How it works
You choose **3 courses (12 credits)** in total. You can either:
- Pick a **thematic bundle** below and go deep into one exciting area (great if you already know what you love), **or**
- Mix and match from the big list of **General Programme Electives** to build your own combination.

There is no "wrong" choice here. Follow your curiosity, and remember: the goal is to *build things* and learn by doing.
