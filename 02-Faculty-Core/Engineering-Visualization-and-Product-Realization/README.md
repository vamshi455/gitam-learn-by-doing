# Engineering Visualization and Product Realization (MECH1011)

**Category:** Faculty Core &middot; **Credits:** 2 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 0-0-4-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject teaches you how to turn an idea in your head into a clear drawing, and then into a real object. You learn engineering drawing (the neat, precise way engineers sketch parts), computer-aided design (CAD software for making 3D models), and how to build a prototype. It is all about seeing a design from every angle before you make it.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Before any phone, chair, or car is built, someone draws and models it on a computer. CAD skills are used in factories, architecture, gaming, and 3D printing. Being able to visualize objects in 3D and communicate designs clearly is valuable everywhere, and it pairs well with AI tools that now help generate and test designs.

## 🎯 A practical real-world problem to learn it
Design a custom phone stand and model it in 3D. Start by sketching your idea on paper with correct measurements, thinking about the angle that holds a phone comfortably and a slot for the charging cable. Then rebuild your sketch inside free CAD software as a 3D model you can spin around and inspect. If you have access to a 3D printer, you can even print it and actually use it on your desk. This one project takes you all the way from a rough idea, to a proper drawing, to a real product you made yourself.

## 🛠️ What you'd build or try
- Sketch a phone stand on paper with real measurements and views.
- Rebuild it as a 3D model in free CAD software.
- Rotate and check the model to fix problems before making it.
- Prototype it with cardboard or a 3D printer and test if it holds a phone.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Engineering drawing and standard views
- CAD software basics
- 3D modeling of real parts
- Visualizing objects from multiple angles
- Prototyping an idea into a real object
