# Universal Human Values 2: Understanding Harmony (HSMCH102)

**Category:** Faculty Core &middot; **Credits:** 3 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 2-1-0-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject is a gentle look inward and outward. It helps you understand yourself, what truly makes you happy, and the values that guide a good life. It then widens out to harmony with others — family, friends, society, and nature. It is less about facts to memorize and more about thinking honestly about how to live well and treat people kindly.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
No matter how skilled you become, life and work go better when you understand yourself and get along with others. Teams, families, and communities thrive on trust, respect, and care. Even in technology and AI, we constantly face questions of ethics — what is fair, what is right, who gets helped or harmed — so a strong sense of values makes you a wiser builder and a better person.

## 🎯 A practical real-world problem to learn it
Keep a "Harmony Journal" for two weeks. Each day, quietly observe your own feelings and one interaction with another person, and write a few honest lines about it. Notice moments when you felt truly happy versus just briefly excited. Notice when you felt respected or when you respected someone else, and when there was tension. At the end, read back through your notes and look for patterns: What brings you real, lasting happiness? What builds trust with others? This is a reflection exercise, not code, and it helps you actually feel the ideas of this course instead of only reading them.

## 🛠️ What you'd build or try
- Each day, note one honest feeling about yourself and one interaction with someone.
- Mark moments of lasting happiness versus short-lived excitement.
- Write down times you felt trust, respect, or tension with others.
- After two weeks, review your notes and find your own patterns and lessons.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Self-awareness and knowing what makes you happy
- Understanding your core values
- Ethics and doing what is right
- Harmony in relationships and society
- Living in harmony with nature
