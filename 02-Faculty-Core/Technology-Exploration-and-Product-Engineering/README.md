# Technology Exploration and Product Engineering (MECH1041)

**Category:** Faculty Core &middot; **Credits:** 2 &middot; **L-T-P-S-J:** 0-0-4-0-0

## 🧠 What this subject is about
This subject is about the journey of turning a good idea into a real, useful product. You learn design thinking (a friendly way to solve problems by understanding people's needs), how to build quick prototypes, and the step-by-step process engineers follow from idea to finished thing. It is hands-on and creative, and there are no wrong first attempts.

## 🌍 Why it matters in the real world
Every product you love started as a rough idea that someone shaped, tested, and improved. Companies use design thinking to invent new gadgets, apps, and services. Learning to prototype fast and improve from feedback is exactly how startups and inventors work, and it is a mindset that helps you build AI-powered products people actually want.

## 🎯 A practical real-world problem to learn it
Invent a simple gadget that solves an annoyance in your daily life. Maybe it is a holder that keeps your earphones from tangling, a reminder clip for your water bottle, or a stand that keeps your book open while you eat. Start by watching the problem closely and asking others if they face it too. Then sketch a few ideas, pick one, and build a rough prototype from cardboard, tape, or household items. Show it to friends, listen to their feedback, and make a better version. This full loop — spot a need, design, build, test, improve — is the real heart of product engineering.

## 🛠️ What you'd build or try
- Notice a small everyday problem and describe who has it.
- Sketch three possible solutions and pick the best one.
- Build a rough prototype from cardboard or simple materials.
- Get feedback from friends and make an improved version.

## 📚 Key ideas you'll practice
- Design thinking and understanding user needs
- Turning ideas into sketches
- Rapid prototyping with simple materials
- The engineering design process
- Improving a product from feedback
